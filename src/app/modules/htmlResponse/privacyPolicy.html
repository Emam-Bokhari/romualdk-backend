GoConnecte — Privacy Policy

Your privacy and data security are important to us.

GoConnecte is a car rental platform that connects vehicle owners (Hosts) with users who want to rent cars. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use our mobile application and services.

1. Information We Collect

  • User Information: Name, phone number, email address, profile image, and basic account details.
  • Host Verification Information: Driving license, national ID (NID), vehicle registration documents, and related verification data collected strictly for security and compliance purposes.
  • Vehicle Information: Car details, car photos, registration number, and availability information provided by Hosts.
  • Booking & Rental Data: Rental dates, pickup and drop-off locations, booking history, and communication related to rentals.
  • Device & Usage Data: Device type, IP address, app usage patterns, and crash or diagnostic reports.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Verify Host identity and vehicle authenticity for platform safety.
  • Enable users to browse, book, and rent vehicles.
  • Manage role switching between User and Host accounts.
  • Process bookings, payments, and rental activities.
  • Improve platform performance, security, and user experience.
  • Send important notifications, updates, and service-related messages.

3. Role Flexibility

On GoConnecte, a user may choose to become a Host and switch roles within the same account. Data collected for each role is used only for its intended purpose and handled with strict access control.

4. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties. Your data is shared only with:

  • Trusted payment and verification service providers
  • Legal or regulatory authorities when required by law
  • Internal teams for fraud prevention, security, and support

5. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures including encryption, access control, and secure infrastructure to protect your information. While we take strong precautions, no digital system can be guaranteed 100% secure.

6. Your Privacy Controls

  • Update or edit your profile information at any time
  • Switch between User and Host roles
  • Request account deletion through in-app support
  • Control notification and communication preferences

7. Children’s Privacy

GoConnecte is not intended for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

8. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any significant changes will be communicated through the app or via email.
