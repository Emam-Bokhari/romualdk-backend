How to Delete Your Account

Follow the steps below to permanently delete your account from the app.

  1. Open the app. From the Home Page, navigate to your Profile section.
  2. Open the Left Sidebar. Scroll to find the Delete Account section.
  3. Tap the Delete Account button. A confirmation modal will appear where you must enter your password.
    Once confirmed, your account will be permanently deleted.
